## Differences between the drafts

The article disclosure categorically excludes AI contributions to mathematical work and limits assistance to language editing and minor LaTeX adjustments. The proposal disclosure additionally allows marginal speculative brainstorming while affirming scientific autonomy and responsibility. The email disclosure states that the message was written by the author and limits any AI assistance to grammar checking and linguistic refinement; it does not mention LaTeX. The website footer covers language editing and HTML/PHP assistance, does not mention LaTeX, and links to the declaration and the aruba source branch. The email and website disclosures describe possible assistance rather than claiming that every message or page used AI.
