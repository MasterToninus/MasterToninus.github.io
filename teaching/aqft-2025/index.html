Program

The course introduces the algebraic approach to Quantum Field Theory on flat Lorentzian spacetimes. We start from configurations and observables as elements of abstract algebras and recall microlocal tools (wavefront sets) to characterise admissible observables. We then discuss generalised Lagrangians and explain how the free Lagrangian determines the Poisson structure and the perturbative implementation of the classical interaction picture. Finally, we quantise the free theory via a non-commutative star product, outline the quantum interaction picture through retarded products and Bogoliubov’s formula, and present the Epstein–Glaser renormalisation scheme for a mathematically rigorous construction.
