Wedding list (sort of)

First of all, thank you. If you found your way here, it means you thought of us — and that alone is already a wonderful gift.

The main idea is simple: we’re missing everything, but we don’t really need anything. Or better: we’re missing a lot, but we don’t yet know where to put it.

We’re missing everything, but don’t really need anything

As you may know, we live in the glorious existential condition known as academic precarity: beautiful equations, many suitcases, but no permanent home.

We still don’t know where we’ll be living in the coming years (Rome? France? Some yet-to-be-discovered differentiable manifold), so making a “proper” wedding list feels right now like a rather abstract exercise — even for us.

In short: we’re missing everything, but we don’t know yet where to put it.

Our (non-)trousseau

Sooner or later a real home will come, hopefully with an oven that isn’t just a microwave and a bookshelf that isn’t a pile of moving boxes. When that moment arrives, we’ll have to face the rest: furniture, appliances, kitchen stuff, and that mysterious category of objects you only realize you need when you don’t have them.

For that reason, if you really wish to offer us a gift, the most useful thing would be something flexible — something we can “materialize” once we actually know where and how we’ll be living.

If you really want to give us a gift…

It’s absolutely not required — the greatest gift is your presence, your hugs, your conversations, and (possibly) some questionable dancing at the party. But if you’d still like to make a tangible gesture, here are the options that help us most:

  • IKEA Gift Cards — for the time when we’ll face the furniture problem and discover that our real home is not exactly isomorphic to the IKEA catalogue.
    IKEA Gift Cards
  • Amazon Gift Cards or similar — for appliances, kitchen tools, and various items that statistically break within six months of every move.
    Amazon Gift Cards

Any other gift card or equivalent is perfectly fine — the idea is to adapt everything later to whatever our future (and mysterious) home will be.

Thank you

Whatever you decide, we’re just so happy to have you celebrating with us. The rest — houses, furniture, appliances, and various dishes — will come in time.
